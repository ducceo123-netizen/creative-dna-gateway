## Safe conclusions supported by this sample

1. Onepage uses an **editorial narrative** (reasons to want/use/gift), physically accurate construction/function details, gift/occasion relevance and practical buyer expectations as **distinct content roles**. This does not independently reveal image-level banner style.
2. Product family materially changes the honest action: a mat is approached/stepped on, pillow held/hugged, ornament hung, key hanger reached for, plaque displayed, apparel worn. Image concepts MUST be chosen by product function and physical affordance, not by a universal front-facing product pose.
3. Banner typography treatment cannot be benchmarked from text-only pages: retain existing shared brand DNA and the user's supplied direct visual counterexamples as evidence (industrial solid slab; then insufficient contrast), and inspect actual desktop/mobile full-size banner pixels before approving any new visual pattern.
4. No claim that a given current PawfectHouse banner uses a certain gradient, crop, photography style or font can be inferred without visual evidence. Do not automatically mark a brand reference 'approved' from a source URL alone.

## Banner / camera-direction standard (an applied production policy, not a claim of observed live banner pixels)

- **Truth before camera**: actual user orientation, hands/fingers/wrist/elbow, eye-line to action, gravity/contact, dimensions and product mechanism are higher priority than every design being fully visible.
- **Moment vs proof**: emotional Banner/Occasion/WYL/UGC can show partly obscured artwork. Provide a separate real-world close-up/over-shoulder/high-angle/top-down/Product Details image when design readability is required.
- **Typography**: natural copy-space and a restrained feathered transparent local support layer if needed; not a flat visible wall/solid slab, and not unreadable floating type. Inspect each viewport independently.
- **Brand lock**: preserve fixed Onepage UI layout/slot dimensions, approved copy, source PDP design diversity, tight approved EO icon language, existing CTA restriction and current QA gates.
- **Source tagging**: future approved banner pattern must store direct page/asset URL, capture date, viewport, exact examined pixels or screenshot, product role, what was actually observed, and who approved; otherwise mark as unverified hypothesis.
