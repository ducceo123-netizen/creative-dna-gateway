## Required production standard

### A. Integrated banner creative
Each aspect ratio is generated as **one complete final banner image containing both scene and final typography**. No blank-scene-then-independent-text compositing default. Do not merely paste generic type onto a stock photograph. Desktop: **2560×960**. Mobile: **720×700**. Both use identical approved campaign title, product, printable artwork and human story, but independently composed camera/crop and optical type hierarchy.

### B. PFH EO typo and contrast
A credible PFH banner feels art-directed, not templated: appropriate expressive title face/treatment for that product category, correct spelling, meaningful breaks, emphatic but elegant scale, short approved support only where warranted, consistent line rhythm. **A localized feathered soft blur beneath typography is retained by default** to help legibility. It must be integrated into generated output and disappear optically into scene light: no rectangular solid panel, over-darkened artificial wall, visible semi-opaque stripe, indiscriminate blur fog or sharply cut gradient. Verify actual text vs busy environment on desktop and mobile.

### C. Real product scene
Start from verified PDP, exact design, physical mechanism and ordinary customer action. Cast, hands, eye-line, gravity, camera and subject gesture must make sense. Do not rotate, stretch, paste or reverse product to get a full design toward the camera; when visibility matters create a separate verified close-up / elevated / over-shoulder detail asset in its appropriate section.

### D. Whole-page diversity and sections
Onepage needs honest design variety across Banner/WYL/Occasion/UGC; don't count a different angle/name/context of the same print as a new design. Product Details uses one real anchor SKU with distinct factual closeups. Content must come from actual PDP/gallery/variants and buyer concerns. Choose Good To Know vs FAQs based on real product insight; avoid generic repeated benefit claims. First-pass EO icons share a verified badge/line-weight reference and undergo a small-size consistency review.

### E. Fail conditions
Reject: unreadable title, wrong spelled headline, bland default fonts, mechanical line break, blurry tiny support, typographic collisions, visible solid slab, excessive local blur, artificial blank wall, unrelated occasion, mismatched desktop/mobile story, product physical impossibility, wrong artwork/spec, repeated design pool, clumsy icon glyphs, missing final overall image or checkbox UI in production FAQ.
