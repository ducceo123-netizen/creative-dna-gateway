Frequently Asked Questions
Choose the name-only design or the photo-and-name design. Available backgrounds and personalization fields depend on the design you select.
Measure your closed, packed gift box, allowing extra paper for folds and overlap. The measurements below are paper width × length.

Size | Inches | Centimeters
S | 11.7 × 16.5 in | 29.7 × 42 cm
M | 16.5 × 23.4 in | 42 × 59.4 cm
L | 17.7 × 27.6 in | 45 × 70 cm
XL | 29.9 × 36.2 in | 76 × 92 cm
2XL | 29.9 × 72 in | 76 × 183 cm

XL and 2XL have the same width; 2XL offers almost twice the length. Measurements may vary by 0.4–0.6 in (1–1.5 cm).
For the photo design, choose a clear, well-lit image with the face centered and unobstructed. Use a simple background and avoid heavy filters.
Follow the selected design’s input instructions. Both currently allow up to 12 characters; the name-only design requires letters A–Z. Check spelling and preview your personalization before ordering.
Keep it in a cool, dry place away from moisture and excessive heat. Handle gently and avoid unnecessary folding before use.
