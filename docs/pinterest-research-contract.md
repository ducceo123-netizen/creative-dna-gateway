# Pinterest visual reference research

The shared research contract now requests Pinterest research before creative direction or assets, for the actual product, theme/topic and intended US or named European market. This is a separate visual-inspiration ledger under `research.pinterest_research`; it does not expand allowed product-fact evidence categories.

Both `resolve_creative_dna` and `compile_onepage_job` return `pinterest_contract`, `pinterest_validation` and `readiness.pinterest_visual_direction`. Onepage packets include the Pinterest acquisition step before creative synthesis. Canonical brand rules, product claims, content approval, product-pixel and human-review gates are unchanged.

Use the returned input shape. Supply the exact task source URL, concrete product, theme/topic and intended market. The source URL must match the current brief. When the compiler has a theme, it must match too. Market and product-text relevance remain host-reviewed; structural validation is not market-fit proof. Store direct canonical Pinterest Pin URLs after permitted resolution, actual search queries, timestamps and access limitations. Inspect actual pixels before reporting `image_view` observations and an original proposed adaptation. Search snippets, metadata and thumbnails alone do not establish inspection.

Statuses:
- `AWAITING_RESEARCH`: no ledger supplied; initialization and unrelated research remain available.
- `NEEDS_EVIDENCE`: invalid, cross-task, duplicate, future-dated or unsupported inspection claims.
- `PARTIAL`: blocked, unsupported or zero-result research remains explicit. Use only inspected usable IDs, disclose limits and continue independently supported work.
- `STRUCTURALLY_COMPLETE`: shape checks passed, not server verification of browsing, artistic quality or rights.

No server browser, account login, generation, persistent storage, canonical mutation or deployment is introduced. The host performs public research within existing permissions. Pins/saves/search rank never establish demand, sales, exact product specs or commercial licensing. Market fit is a hypothesis; avoid treating US/Europe as one universal aesthetic. Pinterest cannot override the canonical brand style.

Current URL normalization accepts canonical pinterest.com numeric Pin paths; regional hosts and pin.it need normal permitted resolution first. Failed resolution remains a limitation, never an excuse to bypass access restrictions. Observations allow at most five minutes of clock skew. Existing legacy research payloads remain readable and product-research results unchanged, with an explicit pending Pinterest direction status.

Tests cover honest blocked/partial results, snippet-only rejection, source/theme mismatches, future timestamps, duplicate pins, URL boundaries, and both entry points retaining product/approval gates.
